### **3. Simplify findProjectRoot()**

**File:** `DefaultWeb4TSComponent.ts` (lines 210-217)

**Changed FROM:**
```typescript
private findProjectRoot(): string {

  const thisFileDir = path.dirname(currentFileUrl.pathname);
```
