## **📋 PLAN**

**Objective:** Centralize completion output in model using Radical OOP principles

**Requirements Traceability:** User feedback - "why is the output not part of the model"

**Implementation Strategy:**
- **Architecture:** Add `completionOutputLines: string[]` to CLIModel (TypeScript interface)
- **Refactor Pattern:** Replace ALL `console.log('DISPLAY:...')` with `this.model.completionOutputLines.push('DISPLAY:...')`
- **Entry Point:** `shCompletion()` outputs from model, then clears buffer
- **Affected Methods:**
  1. `outputCompletionDiagnostic()` - diagnostic DISPLAY lines
  1. `formatCompletionOutput()` - WORD: and DISPLAY: formatting
  1. `completeMethodName()` - method completions
  1. `completeCommandParameter()` - parameter completions
  1. `cliSignature()` - orchestration (NO output, just coordinates)
  1. `shCompletion()` - ONLY place that reads model and outputs

**Expected Complexity Reduction:**
- BEFORE: 6 methods with console.log scattered throughout
- AFTER: 5 methods PUSH to model, 1 method outputs
- Future features: Just PUSH to model, no method signature changes!
