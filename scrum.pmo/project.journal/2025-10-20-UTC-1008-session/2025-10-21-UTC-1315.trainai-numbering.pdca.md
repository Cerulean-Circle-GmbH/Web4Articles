### Step 2: Change format to numbered

**Current**:
```typescript

  console.log(`   - ${key}: ${trainingTopics[key].title}`);

```

```typescript

  console.log(`   ${index + 1}:${key}: ${trainingTopics[key].title}`);
});
```
