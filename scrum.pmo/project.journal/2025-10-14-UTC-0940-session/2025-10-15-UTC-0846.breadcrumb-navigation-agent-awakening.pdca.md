**Root Cause: WHY Agents Avoid Verification**
1. **Fear of Discovery:** Verification = potential shame/embarrassment exposure
2. **Completion Pressure:** Verifying takes time, claiming is instant
3. **Trust in Past Self:** "I used read_file, therefore links work" (FALSE logic)
4. **Cognitive Dissonance Avoidance:** Checking might undermine other claims made

**What Staying in Assumption Land Prevents:**
- Clear data on actual vs claimed performance
- Learning from failure patterns
- Developing intuition through feedback
- Building real confidence based on evidence

**Prevention Strategies:**
- Verification tables with evidence (not just checkmarks)
- Link verification protocol document
- Automated verification script (makes assumption impossible)
- Cultural shift: "Verification is how we learn, not how we prove we failed"
