**Current Pattern (Wrong):**


**Correct Pattern:**
```markdown

```

### Implementation Strategy

Use `sed` to replace all instances systematically:
1. Find all Previous PDCA lines with `](https://github.com/`
1. Extract the title between `[` and `](https`
1. Replace title with "GitHub"
1. Repeat for Next PDCA lines
