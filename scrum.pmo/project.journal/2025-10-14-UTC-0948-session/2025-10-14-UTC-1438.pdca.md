## **🔧 DO**

**Version Analysis and Cleanup**

**1. Analysis Phase**
```bash
for v in 0.3.11.0 0.3.11.1 0.3.11.2 0.3.11.3; do 
  echo "=== $v ==="; 
  ls components/Web4TSComponent/$v/session/ 2>/dev/null | wc -l; 
done
```
Results: 0.3.11.0: 29 files | 0.3.11.1: 31 files | 0.3.11.2: 31 files | 0.3.11.3: 37 files

**2. Cleanup 0.3.11.1 (29 duplicates deleted, 2 remaining)**
```bash
comm -12 <(ls components/Web4TSComponent/0.3.11.0/session/ | sort) <(ls components/Web4TSComponent/0.3.11.1/session/ | sort) | while read file; do rm -v "components/Web4TSComponent/0.3.11.1/session/$file"; done
```

**3. Cleanup 0.3.11.2 (2 duplicates deleted, 29 remaining)**
