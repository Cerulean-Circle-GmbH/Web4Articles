### **The REAL Issue: What Needs to Change?**

**Current Behavior:**
- Completion IS working on single TAB
- The "🔍 Calculating..." messages appear

**User's Terminal Shows:**
```
web4tscomponent <TAB>
🔍 Calculating completions for: `web4tscomponent `

your web4 command > web4tscomponent compl
🔍 Calculating completions for: `web4tscomponent compl`
```

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Single TAB IS triggering completion
- ✅ Messages ARE appearing
- ❓ But bash might not be showing the completion LIST immediately

**Root Cause:**
The `complete -F ... -o nospace` at line 274 registers the completion, but bash's default behavior for ambiguous completions is:
- **First TAB:** Completes to longest common prefix
- **Second TAB:** Shows all options

**Solution Needed:**
Add `bind 'set show-all-if-ambiguous on'` to **PROJECT ROOT** `source.env`, NOT the component one.
