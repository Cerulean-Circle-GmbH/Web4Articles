### **Learning Extracted**

**Filename Discipline:**
> PDCA filenames are strictly `YYYY-MM-DD-UTC-HHMM.pdca.md`. No exceptions. No "helpful" context. The git history and PDCA content provide context - the filename provides only timestamp.

**Key Insight:** The temptation to add descriptive text like `.cmm3.correction` or `.error` or `.fix` comes from wanting to help future readers. But this violates the discipline. The filename is a pure timestamp - nothing more, nothing less.

**Why Strict Naming Matters:**
1. Tools can parse filenames reliably (no special cases)
1. Sorting works correctly (chronological order)
1. No naming decisions to make (just timestamp)
1. Git history shows what changed (read commit, not filename)
