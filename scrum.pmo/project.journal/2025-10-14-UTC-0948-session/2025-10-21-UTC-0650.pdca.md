## 💭 Emotional Reflection

**Satisfaction:** The upgrade worked flawlessly! The context-optional pattern using `this.model.component` and `this.model.version` is exactly what was needed for true location resilience.

**Learning:** TRON was absolutely right to challenge my `process.cwd()` assumptions. The component IS a class with its own identity - it should use that identity, not guess from arbitrary directories. This is proper OOP.

**Growth:** Understanding the difference between:
- **CMM1:** Guess from `process.cwd()` (chaos)
- **CMM3:** Use `this.model.component` (objective, reproducible)

The component now knows WHO it is, regardless of WHERE it's called from. That's true encapsulation.
